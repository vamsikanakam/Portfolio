01 / The challenge I worked on

Finished-good quality varied—even when the oven followed one standard profile.

The project began with only around 60% consistency in finished-good parameters, against a target of 95%+. Finished-good quality was assessed across several attributes. The three priority checks were moisture (~1.5–2%), stack height (90–96 mm for 20 biscuits), and visual appearance/dimensions, including biscuit thickness (4.5–4.8 mm per biscuit). Other checks included texture, bite, diameter, and visible defects such as holes, puffiness and dark edges. Upstream variation in ingredients and mixing was not consistently linked to oven-profile adjustments, leaving operators to rely on experience and manual changes.

03 / Calibrate the model

I led the calibration workflow around golden-sample values and structured sensitivity analysis to tune the baking profile.

Baseline
Establish the reference condition

Used lab/ideal golden-sample parameters to define the reference for model calibration and establish a baseline oven profile for sensitivity testing.

Inputs
Bring process variables into the model

Worked with inputs including initial moisture, biscuit composition, oven temperature, water content, ammonium bicarbonate (ABC) content, baking time, oven air velocity, damper extraction percentage, specific heat capacity and belt speed.

Calibration
Compare predicted results with the golden sample

Managed the calibration process against lab/ideal golden-sample values, coordinating design of experiments (DOE) and sensitivity analysis with the project team and Siemens.

Validation
Run sensitivity analysis over one month

Coordinated a month-long sensitivity-analysis effort across interacting parameters to identify profile settings that kept quality parameters within tolerance.

Why this mattered

Previously, one standard oven profile could be used despite changes in ingredients and pre-mixing. This left upstream variation insufficiently connected to baking-profile decisions. The calibration effort was intended to make profile recommendations responsive to process inputs and required finished-good quality—not simply repeat a historical setting.
